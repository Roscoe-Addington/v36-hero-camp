# Continuum: rules for this project

- **Users:** Continuum primarily supports psychologists in their clinical
  work (patients 16 and older). Medicines are prescribed by a physician.
- **Who performs a treatment:** medicines and medical procedures are marked
  "Prescribed by a physician"; blood tests, ECG and monitoring "Medical
  check (physician or nurse)". Everything without a badge is performed by
  the psychologist or by anyone the psychologist sees fit to perform the
  task. Use this wording.
- **Reviews:** wherever content needs expert review, write "psychiatrist or
  psychologist", never only "psychiatrist".
- **Languages:** everything added is in both Swedish and English. In
  Swedish mode only Swedish is shown (tests check this); article titles,
  journal names and method names stay in their original language.
- **Name:** the app is called "Continuum" (not translated).
- **Access:** only the treating clinician, earlier treating clinicians
  (read-only), named supervisors/close colleagues (read-only) and
  administrators (read-only) see a patient. Emails only say "results ready"
  with a link. GDPR retention applies.
- **Treatments:** follow Socialstyrelsen's national guidelines (priority and
  recommendation id) where they exist; say where NICE differs. Nothing from
  the research watch enters the disorder information without approval by a
  research reviewer.
- **Research sources** (approved 2026-11-15): PubMed, Europe PMC,
  ClinicalTrials.gov, 22 priority journals, and a yearly manual check of
  SBU, Socialstyrelsen, Kunskapsstyrning vård and Läkemedelsverket. Ask
  before adding sources.
- Run `npm test` before committing; update README.md and
  docs/VALIDATION-PLAN.md with every clinical or access change.
